# Device evidence status

`auto` now conservatively selects CPU when qualified evidence is absent. This is an intentional behavior change shared with existing `asr-srt` and Drive callers. Explicit CPU/MPS remain authoritative. The desktop GPU label maps to MPS. Only recognized MPS compatibility failures allow a single automatic CPU fallback; unrelated input, output and unknown failures do not switch devices.

Profiles identify hardware model, physical RAM, OS, Python/FunASR/torch/torchaudio/transformers/modelscope/tiktoken versions, immutable snapshot (including auxiliaries), recognition language/hotwords, cold-per-file loading, planner, policy version and duration bucket. Short (<10 minutes), medium (10–60 minutes) and long (>60 minutes) evidence never mix. Old or incomplete identities do not qualify.

Selection balances comparable source hashes and repetition counts. Each device needs at least three successful, uncached, non-resumed, non-fallback samples of at least 30 seconds. Compare median model-loading plus inference cost; GPU must improve by at least 10%. Unknown quality, timestamps, resources or package identity cannot pass.

Each disposable worker records its own RSS high-water mark and periodic system pressure/swap observations. RSS must stay under 70% of physical RAM. Pressure must remain normal, and swap growth over 64 MiB at two observations at least five seconds apart excludes the sample. Background load is deliberately conservative. [Apple's XNU pressure handler](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/main/bsd/kern/kern_memorystatus_notify.c) converts the internal pressure value to dispatch flags before exposing this sysctl; observed 1/2/4 are interpreted as normal/warning/critical, not the internal enum. An unavailable measurement remains unknown. RSS is not a complete measurement of GPU unified memory.

Calibration is explicitly requested, creates CPU/GPU jobs with the same source and frozen settings, and runs in the normal serial queue. It never secretly reruns accepted media. Cancellation and pause behave like transcription. A known MPS failure quarantines that exact environment/model combination for auto selection until an explicit successful GPU calibration. It does not alter an explicit device request.

**Current limitation:** quality baselines from a reviewed reference corpus are not established. The implementation stores ordinary job observations with `quality_ok=false` and `timestamps_ok=false` for selection purposes; a successful job or calibration cannot bless itself as a quality baseline. Thus current real observations cannot automatically promote GPU. Policy decision tests cover qualified and unqualified traces, but test fixtures are not release performance evidence. The public short-example model smoke proves packaged compatibility only.
